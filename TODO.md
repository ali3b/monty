# TODO

A remake of Montezuma's Revenge (Parker Brothers, 1984) in Phaser 3, with every sprite, tile and ground surface
generated by Synty Sprite Generator. The design is in `docs/DESIGN.md`, the generator gaps in `FEEDBACK.md`.

- [x] **G0 · The temple stands.** Vite + TS + Phaser 3 on port 5197, the asset pipeline (`ssg.assets.json` →
      `npm run assets` → `public/assets/manifest.json`), Joe rendered and walking in the entrance hall, the
      ground tileset driving the walls and the ledges.
- [x] **G1 · The tomb is a tomb.** Ladders and ropes, one-way ledges, fire pits, crumbling floors, dark rooms and
      the torch, three colours of door and key, the five pockets, jewels that never use one, the skull and the
      skeleton, the sword, and the fall that kills.
- [x] **G2 · The pyramid goes down.** Eleven rooms joined by doors, checked by `scripts/check-rooms.mjs`; the idol
      ends the level, the bonus room follows, and every level deeper adds the creatures and the darkness that are
      written down for it.
- [x] **G3 · It is a game, not a diorama.** Title, level cards, HUD, sounds, lives, the game over screen and a
      high score; `room`, `flow`, `deaths` and `gameover` all drive the real game in a browser.
- [ ] **G4 · More tomb, more levels.** Rooms 12+ so a level is a real descent rather than a room with a choice in
      it: a second storey over the entry hall, a water room, a conveyor, a phase-in platform, and the spider and
      snake that the library is missing (FB-003).
- [ ] **G5 · The rest of the arcade original.** The amulet (a few seconds of harmless creatures), the bats that
      Parker Brothers cut, and the conveyor belt.

## Ideas that fell out of the work

- Joe's ko is a martial-arts clip, so his death reads well, but there is no "caught by a skull" and no "burning
  rope" — both want frames the library would have to supply.
- The `bob` template on the pickups is a stand-in for the original's constant blinking. A `blink` template (on,
  off, on) would make the treasure read as *pick me up*.
- The rooms are ASCII in `src/rooms.ts` and `scripts/make-rooms.mjs` prints them from a compact spec. With more
  rooms than eleven, the generator would earn its keep again.
